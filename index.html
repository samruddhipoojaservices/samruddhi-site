Meet the founder

Rajasekhar Sharma Chintapalli

Hindu Priest · Founder of Samruddhi

I'm a practicing Hindu priest — not a typical web agency. For years, I watched temples struggle with web designers who didn't understand our needs and wouldn't answer when we needed them most. A festival announcement that couldn't wait. A donation page broken on the busiest day of the year.

So I decided to take a chance. I started learning — and with the help of modern AI tools, I built our own temple's website exactly the way a temple should have it. It worked. Devotees noticed. Other temples started asking.

Now I build for temples across the country. Because I stand in the temple every week, I understand what a temple actually needs — and I design every site around that, not around a template. When you call, I answer.

— Rajasekhar
Founder, Samruddhi Pooja Services
